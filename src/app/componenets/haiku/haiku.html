Haiku Challenge

No rebus? That's right. This is a challenge to create your own haiku about bees 🐝.

The 5-7-5 Structure

A traditional English haiku is composed of three lines with a specific syllable count:

  • Line 1: 5 syllables
  • Line 2: 7 syllables
  • Line 3: 5 syllables

Key Elements

  • The "Cutting Word" (Kireji): In Japanese, this is a grammatical marker that creates a pause or a shift in the poem. In English, we usually achieve this with a comma, dash, or a simple break in thought between lines.
  • The "Seasonal Reference" (Kigo): Most haikus include a word that hints at the time of year (e.g., "blossoms" for spring or "frost" for winter).
  • Simplicity: Avoid flowery adjectives. Focus on a single, vivid image.

Example: The Honey Bee

Golden wings in flight, (5)
Dancing through the summer heat, (7)
Dusty legs with gold. (5)
